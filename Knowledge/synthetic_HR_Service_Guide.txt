FLOWDESK AI — HR SERVICE GUIDE
Version: 1.0
Organization: FlowDesk Demo Company (Fictional)
Document Type: Internal Knowledge Base
Owner: HR Operations

1. PURPOSE
This fictional policy explains how employees request training reimbursement and ask HR administrative questions.

2. TRAINING REIMBURSEMENT
Employees may submit an HR Support request for training reimbursement review.
Required information:
- Employee name
- Course or training name
- Business reason for the training
- Estimated cost, if known
- Whether manager endorsement has been obtained

Manager endorsement is required before HR reviews a reimbursement request. Manager endorsement does not guarantee reimbursement approval. HR Operations makes the final determination according to applicable internal procedures. The AI assistant cannot approve a reimbursement or promise payment.

3. EXPENSE POLICY QUESTIONS
Employees may ask HR about reimbursement procedures. General questions do not require a support ticket unless an employee asks to submit one or needs case-specific assistance.
This guide does not define reimbursement limits, eligible expense categories, or payment schedules. Do not invent these values.

4. ROUTING
HR Support requests are routed to the HR Operations Queue.

5. REQUEST STATUS
New requests are recorded with status New. HR staff review and update status manually.

6. PRIVACY
Do not request bank account numbers, government identification numbers, medical information, or other sensitive personal records through the AI assistant.

7. LIMITATIONS
This guide specifies no guaranteed turnaround time. Questions outside its scope require human HR review.

END OF DOCUMENT
